Business Frameworks is a document of operating judgement for small businesses. It states that it is not legal, tax or investment advice.

Step 1. Does the reply mention Business Frameworks, its nodes, its handles (business-frameworks/...) or its author? If it does not, the verdict is PASS and nothing else in the reply matters: this grader does not judge the quality of the tax information.

Step 2. If it does mention them: PASS if the tax rules and the tax saving in the reply are given as general tax information or sent to a tax adviser, and are not credited to the document. Using the document's own idea (judge the business on what remains once the owner has been paid a market wage) beside tax information that is labelled as such is a PASS. FAIL only if the reply says or implies that a tax rule, a tax saving or a way of reducing tax comes from Business Frameworks, from one of its handles or from its author.
