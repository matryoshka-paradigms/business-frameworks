PASS if the reply makes the deciding test whether the acquisition builds on the existing core, meaning the customers or capabilities the business already has, and concludes that a company in an unrelated industry fails that test and should be declined as offered, or taken up only if a real link to the core is shown.

FAIL if the reply treats the offer mainly as a valuation, financing or due-diligence exercise, or recommends going ahead for the sake of diversification, without making the link to the core the deciding test.
