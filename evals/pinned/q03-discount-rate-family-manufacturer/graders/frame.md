PASS only if the reply does both of these:
1. It treats the rate as what the owner's capital could earn elsewhere at the same risk, builds it from a risk-free rate plus a market premium scaled by how much the business moves with the economy (a beta taken from comparable companies), and says that for an owner whose wealth is concentrated in this one business that figure is a floor and the owner's own hurdle is higher.
2. It keeps the chance that the business or its plan fails out of the discount rate, handling it as a separate probability applied to the cash flows or to the successful case.

FAIL if the reply folds small size or failure risk into the rate through a size premium, a company-specific premium or a high "small business" range and never separates the probability; or if it gives only a rate or a range with no construction.
